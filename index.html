Background & philosophy

Trained in code.
Proven in operations.

Two degrees, two countries and one consistent thread: finding where things get stuck and building a better flow.

I started in computer science in Dhaka, learned operations by doing them, and moved to London to study how global supply chains actually work.

At Photolab Trading I managed orders from supplier purchase order to customer delivery. At QuickTech IT Solutions I kept 30+ client projects on schedule, and as co-founder and COO of dev.resolved I ran the operations of a growing tech venture. Running Ace Events taught me the unglamorous side of logistics: suppliers, staffing and on-the-day problems that don't wait. In the UK I led operations for a busy restaurant group, handling stock, deliveries and rotas, while completing my MSc at the University of Hull.

In September 2026 I co-founded Scrabbl Technologies with three partners. As CEO, I bring the operations mindset to software: understand the process first, then build the tool that removes the friction.

Good supply chains are invisible. My job is to make the work behind them visible, measurable and better.
