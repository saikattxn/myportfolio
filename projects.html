MSc · AnalyticsR

Retail sales drivers

Problem
Which economic and seasonal factors actually move weekly sales across a 45-store retail chain?
What I did
Modelled 6,435 weekly observations from 2010 to 2012 with regression, testing CPI, unemployment, temperature and fuel price as predictors, and checked the model diagnostics.
Result
A full analytical report with visualisations and diagnostics, setting out which factors had a measurable effect on sales and how far each could support demand forecasting.

MSc · Trade & logisticsCIF

EV battery import model

Problem
Importing lithium-ion EV batteries from China to the UK means dangerous-goods rules, post-Brexit customs and costs that are easy to underestimate.
What I did
Modelled a CIF shipment end to end: Incoterms® 2020 responsibilities, IMDG hazardous goods compliance, UK customs requirements and the total landed cost.
Result
A complete import plan and landed cost breakdown showing a UK buyer the real cost and compliance steps behind each shipment.
Incoterms® 2020IMDG CodeLanded costCustoms
MSc · DissertationSLR

Resilient EV battery supply

Problem
How can the UK electric vehicle industry secure lithium-ion battery supply chains that are both resilient and sustainable?
What I did
Ran a systematic literature review following the PRISMA 2020 protocol, built on 32 peer-reviewed empirical studies and 5 grey literature sources, and coded the evidence thematically.
Result
A qualitative framework of resilience and sustainability strategies for the UK EV battery supply chain, grounded in the reviewed evidence.
