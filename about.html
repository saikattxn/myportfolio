Background & philosophy

Trained in code.
Proven in operations.

Two degrees, two countries and one consistent thread: finding where things get stuck and building a better flow.

I started in computer science in Dhaka, learned operations by doing them, and moved to London to study how global supply chains actually work.

At Photolab Trading I handled around 200 orders a month at 90% on-time delivery. At QuickTech IT Solutions I delivered 30+ client projects, 95% of them on schedule, and as co-founder and COO of dev.resolved I ran the operations of a software development venture. Running Ace Events taught me the unglamorous side of logistics: suppliers, staffing and on-the-day problems that don't wait. In the UK I lead operations at Tiger Lily Limited, a busy restaurant business, where tighter stock control has cut food waste by 5%, alongside completing my MSc at the University of Hull.

Together with four like-minded partners, I founded Scrabbl Technologies, a software studio whose engineering team builds from Bangladesh, and in September 2026 we registered it in the UK. As CEO, I bring the operations mindset to software: understand the process first, then build the tool that removes the friction. I run Scrabbl alongside my career: my core path is supply chain and operations.

Good supply chains are invisible. My job is to make the work behind them visible, measurable and better.
